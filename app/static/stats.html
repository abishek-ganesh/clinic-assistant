Usage

Every request to this app writes one row to a log: how long it took, how many tokens it used and what the code decided. This page counts those rows. Watching a running system through numbers like these is called observability.

At a glance

Requests

Every call to any part of the app, refused ones included. Traffic is the baseline: a spike or a sudden silence is often the first sign that something changed.

Est. cost this month

Tokens used times the generative model's price. When the month's total reaches the cap the app stops answering, so a bug or a bot cannot run up a bill.

Median answer time

Half of answered questions finish faster than the median, and 95 in 100 finish faster than the 95th percentile (p95). An average hides the slow ones, so engineers watch the slow end.

Decline rate

The share of questions the app chose not to answer. A jump means the handbook has gaps or a threshold is too strict. Near zero can mean it answers things it should not.

Requests per day

One bar per day, in Eastern time. The shape shows when the app is used, and an unusual day stands out against its neighbors.
