# Appointments

This section explains how to book, change and cancel a visit at Family Clinic, and what to expect when you arrive.

## How to book

There are two ways to book an appointment:

- **Patient portal:** returning patients can book, reschedule and cancel online at any time of day.
- **Phone:** call (555) 555-0100 during phone line hours. New patients must book their first visit by phone so we can set up a chart.

We do not book appointments by email or by voicemail. Routine visits are usually available within 2 weeks. Annual physicals often book 4 to 6 weeks out, so plan ahead.

## Visit lengths

- New patient visit: 40 minutes
- Returning patient visit: 20 minutes
- Annual physical: 40 minutes

If you have more than two concerns to discuss, tell us when you book so we can schedule a longer visit. A returning patient visit can be extended to 40 minutes when it is requested in advance.

## Same-day appointments

Each location holds a small number of same-day appointments for returning patients who need to be seen quickly. Same-day slots are released at 7:30 am, Monday to Friday, and can only be booked by phone. They usually fill within the first hour, so call as close to 7:30 am as you can. Same-day visits may be with any available provider, not necessarily your usual one.

## Cancelling or rescheduling

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. You can cancel through the patient portal or by phone. A voicemail left on the main line counts as notice, as long as it is left at least 24 hours before your appointment time.

## No-show fee

If you miss an appointment or cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, we charge a $40 no-show fee. Insurance does not cover this fee. The fee is waived once per calendar year, and it is always waived for a documented emergency. After three no-shows in 12 months, you will only be able to book same-day appointments.

## Arriving late

Please arrive on time. If you arrive more than 10 minutes after your appointment time, we may need to reschedule you so that other patients are not kept waiting. When the schedule allows, we will offer a shortened visit or the next open slot that day instead. Being rescheduled for late arrival does not carry the no-show fee.

## What to bring

New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to finish registration. Please bring:

- A photo ID
- Your insurance card
- A list of your current medications
- Your copay, if your plan has one

Returning patients only need an ID and insurance card if something has changed since the last visit.
