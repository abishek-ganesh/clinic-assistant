# Privacy and medical records

This notice describes how Family Clinic uses and protects your health information, and how you can see or get a copy of your medical records.

## How we use your records

We use your health information for three purposes:

- **Treatment:** your care team reads and updates your chart, and shares what is needed with specialists, labs and pharmacies involved in your care.
- **Payment:** we send your insurance plan the details it needs to pay for your visit.
- **Clinic operations:** we review charts to check quality and train staff.

Staff may only open the parts of your record that they need to do their job.

## When we need your permission

For anything outside treatment, payment and clinic operations, we need your written permission first. That includes sharing records with an employer, a school, a lawyer or a life insurance company. We never sell your information or use it for marketing. You can withdraw a permission at any time by telling us in writing.

## Getting a copy of your records

To get a copy of your medical records, fill out a records request form. The form is on the Documents page of the patient portal and at the front desk of either location. We fulfill records requests within 30 days. Electronic copies are free and are delivered through the portal. Paper copies are free for the first 20 pages and $0.25 per page after that. You will need to show a photo ID when you pick up paper copies.

## Sending records to someone else

We can send your records to another doctor, a specialist or a family member if you sign a release form that names the person or office. Releases are processed within 30 days, and usually faster when the request comes from another clinic for ongoing care. A release stays in effect for 12 months unless you cancel it sooner.

## Patient portal access

Patients aged 18 and over get their own patient portal account. In the portal you can see visit summaries, test results, medications, immunization records and statements. Test results are posted within 2 business days of being reviewed by your provider.

Parents and guardians can ask for proxy access to the portal account of a child under 12. For patients aged 12 to 17, proxy access is limited to appointments, immunization records and billing.

## Correcting your records

If you think something in your record is wrong or incomplete, you can ask us to correct it. Send the request in writing to the privacy officer and say what should change and why. We respond within 60 days. If we do not agree with the change, you can add a statement of your own to your record.

## Privacy questions and complaints

Our privacy officer handles questions and complaints about how your information is used. Call (555) 555-0140, Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 4:00 pm, or send a message through the portal and choose the topic "Privacy". You will not be treated differently for filing a complaint. If we learn that your information was seen by someone who should not have seen it, we will notify you in writing.
