# Vaccines and immunizations

Family Clinic gives routine vaccines to children and adults. All vaccines are given at the Main Street location, where the vaccine room is.

## Routine vaccine schedule

We follow the national recommended immunization schedules for children and adults. Childhood vaccines are given at well-child visits, so keeping those visits on schedule keeps vaccines on schedule. For adults, your provider reviews which vaccines are due at each annual physical. You can see which vaccines you have had, and which are coming due, on the Immunizations page of the patient portal.

## Flu shot clinics

Flu shots are available from September 15 each year. We run walk-in flu shot clinics at Main Street on four Saturdays: October 3, October 10, October 17 and October 24, 2026, from 9:00 am to 12:00 pm. No appointment is needed on those days. Flu clinics are open to patients aged 3 and over. Younger children get their flu shot at a regular visit.

## Walk-in or appointment

Flu shots are the only vaccine we give on a walk-in basis. Besides the Saturday flu clinics, you can walk in for a flu shot at Main Street on weekdays between 9:00 am and 11:00 am. Every other vaccine needs an appointment, because doses are ordered and prepared ahead of time. Book a "vaccine visit" through the portal or by phone. Vaccine visits take 15 minutes.

## Vaccine cost and insurance coverage

Most insurance plans, including all the plans we are in-network with, cover routine vaccines in full, with no copay. If you do not have insurance, a flu shot costs $35. Self-pay prices for other vaccines vary, so ask the front desk for the current price list before your visit. A vaccine given during a regular office visit does not add a second copay.

## Vaccines we do not offer

We do not stock travel vaccines. If you need vaccines for an international trip, ask us for a list of local travel clinics, and book with them at least 6 weeks before you leave. Riverside does not give vaccines of any kind.

## Immunization records and school forms

You can download your immunization record from the Immunizations page of the patient portal at any time, free of charge. If you need a form signed by a provider, such as a school, camp or employer form, drop it off at the front desk or upload it through the portal. Signed forms are ready within 5 business days. If you had vaccines somewhere else, bring the record to your next visit and we will add them to your chart.
