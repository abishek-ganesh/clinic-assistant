# Telehealth visits

A telehealth visit is a video appointment with a Family Clinic provider. This section covers who can use it, how to join and what still needs an in-person visit.

## Who can use telehealth

Telehealth is available to returning patients who have been seen in person at Family Clinic within the last 12 months. You must be physically located in the state at the time of the visit, because our providers are licensed here. New patients need an in-person first visit before they can book telehealth.

## What telehealth is good for

Video visits work well for:

- Follow-up visits for ongoing conditions
- Medication check-ins and refill visits
- Reviewing lab or test results
- Questions that do not need a physical exam

Telehealth visits are 20 minutes long, the same as a returning patient visit.

## How to join your visit

Book a telehealth visit through the patient portal or by phone, the same way as an in-person visit. To join, log in to the patient portal 10 minutes before your appointment time, open the Appointments page and choose "Join video visit". There is nothing to download. Your provider will join at the appointment time. If the video has not connected 10 minutes after your start time, we will call the phone number in your chart.

## What cannot be done by video

Some care needs an in-person visit:

- Physical exams, including annual physicals
- Vaccines and other injections
- Lab draws and other tests
- Visits to start or renew a controlled substance
- New patient visits

If your provider decides during a video visit that you need to be seen in person, we will help you book that visit before you hang up.

## Technology you need

- A phone, tablet or computer with a camera and a microphone
- An up-to-date web browser
- A steady internet connection, on wifi or mobile data
- A private, quiet and well-lit place to sit

You can test your camera and microphone at any time from the Appointments page of the portal. Please do not join a visit while driving. If the video fails, the visit can continue as a phone call.

## Cancelling a telehealth visit

Telehealth visits follow the same cancellation rule as in-person visits. Give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule, through the portal or by phone. A missed telehealth visit, or one cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, is charged the same $40 no-show fee.

## Telehealth cost

Most insurance plans cover telehealth visits the same way as office visits, with the same copay. Your copay is charged to the card on file in the portal before the visit starts. The self-pay rate for a telehealth visit is $90.
