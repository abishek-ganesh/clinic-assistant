# Hours and locations

Family Clinic is a primary care practice with two locations: Main Street and Riverside. You can be seen at either one, and your records are available at both.

## Main Street location

The Main Street clinic is at 100 Main Street, Suite 200. It is our larger location and the only one open on Saturdays. The lab draw station and the vaccine room are both here.

Main Street hours:

- Monday to Friday: 8:00 am to 6:00 pm
- Saturday: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm
- Sunday: closed

## Riverside location

The Riverside clinic is at 250 Riverside Drive, on the first floor. It is a smaller office with weekday hours only.

Riverside hours:

- Monday to Friday: 8:00 am to 5:00 pm
- Saturday and Sunday: closed

Riverside does not have a lab draw station. If your provider orders blood work, you will be sent to Main Street for the draw.

## Saturday hours

We are open on Saturdays at the Main Street location only, from 9:00 am to 1:00 pm. Riverside is closed on weekends. Saturday visits are for returning patients and are booked the same way as weekday visits. Both locations are closed on Sundays.

## Holiday closures

Both locations are closed on these holidays:

- New Year's Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day and the Friday after
- Christmas Day

On Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve both locations close early, at 12:00 pm. When a holiday falls on a weekend, the closure moves to the nearest weekday. The after-hours nurse line stays open on every holiday.

## Parking and getting here

Main Street has a free parking lot behind the building. The lot entrance is on Oak Avenue, and there are four accessible spaces next to the rear door. City bus routes 4 and 9 stop in front of the building.

Riverside shares a parking garage with the rest of the building. Bring your garage ticket to the front desk and we will validate it for up to 2 hours. After 2 hours the garage charges its posted rate.

## Phone line hours

Our main phone number for both locations is (555) 555-0100. The phone line is staffed:

- Monday to Friday: 7:30 am to 6:00 pm
- Saturday: 9:00 am to 1:00 pm

Outside those times, the same number offers the option to reach the after-hours nurse line or leave a message. Messages are returned the next business day. The phone line is busiest between 8:00 am and 10:00 am, so call later in the day for shorter waits on routine questions.
