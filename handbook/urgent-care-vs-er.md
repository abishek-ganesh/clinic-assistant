# Urgent care, the emergency room and after-hours help

Family Clinic is a primary care office. We are not an urgent care center or an emergency room. This section explains where to go when you need help quickly and the clinic cannot see you.

## When to call 911

If you think you or someone else is having a medical emergency, call 911 right away. Do not call the clinic first, do not send a portal message and do not wait for a call back. The clinic phone line and the patient portal are not monitored for emergencies. Do not drive yourself to the hospital. If you are not sure whether it is an emergency, treat it as one and call 911.

## When to use the emergency room

The emergency room is for serious problems that cannot safely wait. It is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. If someone needs emergency care and cannot get there safely, call 911 instead of arranging a ride. Family Clinic providers do not work in the emergency room, but we receive a report of your visit and will follow up with you afterwards.

## When to use urgent care

Urgent care centers are for problems that need attention the same day but are not emergencies, when the clinic is closed or has no same-day appointments left. Most urgent care centers take walk-ins and are open evenings and weekends. Family Clinic does not run an urgent care center, so check that the one you choose is in-network with your insurance plan. Urgent care copays are usually higher than a clinic visit and lower than an emergency room visit.

## Try the clinic first for same-day needs

During opening hours, call (555) 555-0100 before going to urgent care. We hold same-day appointments at both locations, and a visit with us costs less and keeps your care in one place. If we cannot see you that day, our staff will tell you so and suggest urgent care.

## After-hours nurse line

When the clinic is closed, a registered nurse is available by phone at (555) 555-0199. The after-hours nurse line is open from 6:00 pm to 8:00 am on weekdays, and all day on weekends and holidays. The nurse can help you decide whether to wait for a clinic appointment, go to urgent care or go to the emergency room. The nurse cannot prescribe medication, order refills or book appointments. The nurse line is not for emergencies: call 911.

## After an urgent care or emergency visit

Please let us know when you have been to urgent care or the emergency room. Book a follow-up visit with your Family Clinic provider within 7 days, and bring any discharge papers you were given. If the visit was at a hospital outside our area, ask them to send the records to Family Clinic, or sign a release form so we can request them.
