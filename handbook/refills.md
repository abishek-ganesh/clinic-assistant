# Prescription refills

This section covers how to request a refill of a medication that a Family Clinic provider already prescribes for you, and how long it takes.

## How to request a refill

There are two ways to request a refill:

- **Patient portal:** open the Medications page, pick the prescription and choose "Request refill".
- **Your pharmacy:** ask your pharmacy to send us a refill request. This is usually the easiest option, because the pharmacy already has your prescription details.

We do not take refill requests by voicemail, and front desk staff cannot approve refills in person. Request your refill when you have about 7 days of medication left.

## Turnaround time

Refill requests take up to 3 business days to process. Business days are Monday to Friday, not counting holidays. A request sent on a Friday afternoon may not be ready until the following Wednesday. Once a refill is approved, we send it directly to your pharmacy and you will see the status change in the portal. Your pharmacy may need extra time to fill it.

## Controlled substances

Controlled substances cannot be refilled through a portal or pharmacy request. They require a visit with your provider at least every 3 months, and the prescription is renewed during that visit. These prescriptions cannot be called in by phone and are not eligible for 90-day supplies. Lost or stolen controlled prescriptions are not replaced early.

## 90-day supplies

If you have taken the same long-term medication for at least 6 months without changes, you can ask for a 90-day supply instead of a 30-day supply. Ask in your refill request or at your next visit. Your provider decides whether a 90-day supply is appropriate, and your insurance plan decides whether it is covered. Mail-order pharmacies usually require 90-day prescriptions.

## When a visit is needed first

A provider can only keep refilling a medication for patients they see regularly. If it has been more than 12 months since your last visit, we will approve one 30-day refill and ask you to book an appointment before the next one. For many medications this refill visit can be a telehealth visit, so you do not need to come in.

## If you run out

If you are completely out of a medication and your refill has not been processed, call (555) 555-0100 during phone line hours and choose the refill option. Staff can mark the request as urgent. Your pharmacist may also be able to provide a short emergency supply for some medications. After hours, the nurse line can advise you on what to do until the clinic opens.
