# Billing and insurance

This section explains which insurance plans Family Clinic accepts, what you pay at a visit, and what to do when a bill arrives.

## Insurance plans we accept

Family Clinic is in-network with these plans:

- Example Health PPO
- Example Health HMO
- Sample Community Plan
- Sample Senior Plan

If your plan is not on this list, we are out-of-network for you. You can still be seen at our self-pay rates, and we will give you an itemized receipt to send to your insurer. Plans change each January, so please confirm that we are still in-network with your plan at the start of every year.

## Copays

Your copay is due at check-in, before you see the provider. The amount is set by your insurance plan and is usually printed on your insurance card. We accept credit cards, debit cards, health savings account cards and cash. We do not accept personal checks at the front desk.

## Self-pay rates

If you do not have insurance, or we are out-of-network for your plan, these self-pay rates apply:

- New patient visit: $180
- Returning patient visit: $120
- Telehealth visit: $90
- Annual physical: $200

Self-pay visits are paid at check-in. Lab tests and vaccines are billed separately. Ask the front desk for a written estimate before any test.

## Payment plans

If your balance is more than $200, you can spread it over 3, 6 or 12 monthly payments with no interest. The minimum payment is $25 a month. Set up a payment plan through the Billing page of the patient portal or by calling the billing office. Uninsured patients can put self-pay visit charges on a payment plan too. A balance on an active payment plan is never sent to collections.

## Reading your statement

Statements are posted to the patient portal and mailed by the 5th of each month. Each line on a statement shows:

- The date of the visit and the service provided
- The full charge
- What your insurance paid, and any adjustment from our contract with your plan
- "Patient responsibility", which is the amount you owe

Payment is due within 30 days of the statement date. If a line says "pending insurance", you do not owe anything for it yet.

## Billing questions

The billing office can be reached at (555) 555-0130, Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 4:00 pm. You can also send a message from the Billing page of the portal, and we reply within 2 business days. Front desk staff can take payments but cannot explain or change charges.

## If you have trouble paying

We offer a sliding-scale discount to patients whose household income qualifies. Applications are available at the front desk and on the portal, and are reviewed within 10 business days. Contact the billing office before your statement is due and we will put the balance on hold while your application is reviewed.
